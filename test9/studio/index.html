Studio

Who we are

A founder-led systems design studio. We shape programmes, policies and learning for institutions in India and internationally.

Dr. Partha Pratim Das

Founder

Assistant Professor, Department of Design, DTU

Partha is a systems designer and educator with roots in civil and environmental engineering. He holds a PhD in Systems and Strategy Design and an M.Des in Industrial Design, both from IIT Delhi. His work spans design education, programme design and research on how people learn. He leads DTU's Design Mentorship programme and is an Innovation Ambassador with the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell.

Akanshya Dutta

Co-founder

Visiting Faculty, Department of Design, DTU

Akanshya is a researcher and strategist who works between data, policy and people. A computer science engineer by training, she brings technical rigour to questions of policy and people. For the Government of Assam, she has worked on policy monitoring, policy drafting and capacity building. Her teaching spans data visualisation and visual research methods. Alongside Think Studio, she is building Drishta, a community for self-awareness through metacognition.

Unfolded Future

Consulting that sustains community impact

Think Studio is the systems design studio of Unfolded Future. Its revenue funds Drishta, a community programme building self-awareness and emotional regulation through metacognition. Visit Drishta
