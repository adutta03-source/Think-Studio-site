The framework

Metacognition Impact Framework

Six abilities, each scored 0–4 on the same scale and each resting on an established, published research tool, so one person's profile can be compared over time.

  • Raw material
  • Metacognition itself
  • Lasting change
  1. NoticeHow widely I pay attention, with all my sensesIngold; sensewalking
  2. ThinkHow deeply I connect what I notice into ideasSOLO taxonomy
  3. KnowHow well I understand how I learn and thinkFlavell; MAI
  4. SteerHow well I plan, check and adjust my thinkingZimmerman; MAI
  5. ReflectHow honestly I question my assumptionsKember; Mezirow
  6. CarryWhether the change shows up later, in what I doKolb

Our standard for calling it impact

  1. Measured, not assumed. Every participant scored before and after.
  2. Scored fairly. Two raters code writing blind to before or after, with agreement of kappa 0.6 or higher.
  3. Large enough to matter. A rise of one level, reported with an effect size.
  4. Still there later. At least half the gain held at three months.

What this pilot can and cannot show

These scores were given afterwards by one rater, from published and edited essays, with no before measure. They show how the framework reads evidence, not a proven effect. The full protocol runs from Drishta's October 2026 gathering and Think Studio's next university cohort, with a comparison group.

Selected work · Programme design

A three-week design bootcamp, run twice.

An intensive introduction to design for school students and first-year undergraduates from any discipline, run by DTU's Department of Design with Tetrix, its student design society.

Setting
Department of Design, Delhi Technological University, with Tetrix, the design community
Role
Dr Partha Pratim Das, faculty coordinator for the bootcamp and for Tetrix
Editions
30 June to 21 July 2025 · 6 to 27 July 2026
  • 2,086+registrations across two editions, from across India
  • About 6 : 1applicants for every one of the 360 places
  • 88%of 2025 participants completed the full three weeks (158 of 180)
  • 18industry speaker sessions, nine in each edition

What comes next. The bootcamp records demand, attendance and finished projects, but not how participants' thinking changed. With 180 participants, adding our Metacognition Impact Framework to the next edition would give the first large, before-and-after measure of a design programme of this kind.

How we work

Four stages, in this order.

We spend real time on the first two stages, so that the design solves the right problem.

  1. Listen

    We meet you and the people the work is for, and learn how things actually run today.

  2. Frame

    We agree in writing what the problem is, who it is for, and how we will know it worked.

  3. Make

    We design, share drafts early, and revise with you rather than presenting a finished surprise.

  4. Hand over

    You get finished files and the guidance to use and maintain them without us.
